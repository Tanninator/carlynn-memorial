01
Who She Was

A little life, full of color

Carli was four and a half years old, and in those four short years she filled our world with more color than most people manage in a lifetime. She was bright and tender-hearted, endlessly curious, and already a gifted little artist — happiest with a paintbrush in her hand and a fresh sheet of paper in front of her.

She was a devoted big sister to baby Leonardo, and the beloved daughter of Cindy and Peter. She left us suddenly on July 12, 2026. What remains — her paintings, her laughter, the way she looked at the world — we hold close, and we gather here to remember it together.

“Every heart she touched is brighter for having known her.”— In loving memory
